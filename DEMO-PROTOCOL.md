# FlowProof: real-world demo protocol

Use this before entering data in the app. The app records a **self-reported observation**, not a certified water measurement.

## Materials

- One working tap and a basin large enough to catch its discharge without overflow.
- A jug with legible volume markings, or a known-volume bottle for counting full fills and measuring the remainder.
- Two comparable items for one completed washing task, the same cleaning material, and a phone to film.

## Before recording

1. Define the task and completion standard in writing. Example: wash one plate of the same size and comparable residue until no visible residue remains.
2. Define the intervention: turn the tap off during scrubbing, then turn it on for rinsing.
3. Place the basin so **all** tap water used during each task is caught. Test with a small pour. If it spills, change the setup.
4. Check that the jug markings are readable on camera. If uncertain, use a known-volume bottle and record full fills plus remainder.
5. Keep the same tap, basin, vessel, soap, number of items, and completion standard for both trials. Differences in residue, scrubbing time and cleanliness remain limitations.

## Record the trials

1. Film a short baseline clip showing the task, basin, final item and measured volume. Record the measured litres and duration immediately.
2. Reset and film the intervention clip under the same conditions. Show the tap off during scrubbing, basin, final item and measured volume.
3. Note any splash or uncollected water. If either trial loses water, the app rejects the comparison; rerun rather than using its litres as reliable.
4. Run the separate fixed-volume control: fill the same known-volume bottle at two different flow rates. Both fills should remain the same volume; only the time changes. Do not waste water solely for the control if the water can be used afterward.

## What to say in the video

“These are two measured trials of the same task. We observed **X** litres in the baseline and **Y** litres with the tap off during scrubbing, a difference of **X − Y** litres in this test. Conditions were self-reported and one pair does not prove the habit will persist or that every household saves this amount.”

If the intervention uses more water, report the negative result. If the trials are not comparable, show the app rejecting or warning about the comparison and rerun the physical test.

## Before submission

- Replace sample values with the actual readings; do not edit clips to imply a measurement the team did not make.
- Keep raw clips and the recorded values so the three-minute edit can be checked.
- Verify the deployed AWS endpoint and the final receipt on camera.
- Do not say the app verifies someone’s identity, certifies the jug, detects unfilmed water loss, or establishes annual savings.
